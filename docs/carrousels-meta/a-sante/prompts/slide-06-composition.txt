SCENE: A wide flat surface of warm off-white hand-made paper, slight deckle texture and very fine grain visible. Casablanca morning light from a north-facing window. The room is implied, not seen.

SUBJECT: Five small unglazed ceramic shallow dishes (each about 6 cm in diameter), aligned in a precisely spaced horizontal row, equal gaps between them. Each dish holds a different raw matter:
- DISH 1 (left) — fine pale white-beige clay (kaolin), softly mounded.
- DISH 2 — pale green-beige bamboo powder, finer than dish 1.
- DISH 3 — small translucent amber-brown shards of pine resin, irregular shapes.
- DISH 4 — a single small puddle of golden translucent oil (jojoba), shallow and still.
- DISH 5 (right) — small flakes of cream-yellow carnauba wax, matte.

Behind each dish, a small handwritten kraft paper label rests faintly visible — but TEXT IS UNREADABLE (it's a material element, not a glyph).

ACTION: Five matters at rest, side by side, presented for honest inspection.

FRAMING: 90° top-down overhead view, 50mm lens, all five dishes parallel to the bottom edge, perfectly spaced. Deep focus across the row (this is a flat lay — everything sharp). Generous negative space above and small band below.

LIGHTING: Soft diffuse morning light from the upper left, even fall-off across the row. Subtle shadows on the right side of each dish, identical in each.

PALETTE: Warm off-white paper background. Five honest earthen accents: pale clay, pale green bamboo, amber resin, golden oil, cream wax. A slow herbarium of beige-to-amber.

STYLE: Editorial flat lay in the spirit of Aesop catalog and Kinfolk magazine. Natural unretouched film aesthetic, subtle Kodak Portra 400 grain, visible paper grain, honest material texture, no studio polish.

MOOD: Restrained transparency. Authority by showing — never by telling. The viewer sees what is, and concludes for herself.

DO NOT INCLUDE: any readable text or glyphs on the labels (kraft paper labels are blurred or face away); watermarks, logos, brand names, signage; reflective gold or chrome dishes (must remain unglazed ceramic, matte); pearlescent, glitter, gloss; HDR or oversharpening; vibrant saturated colors, neon, magenta, cyan; perfume-ad glow, halo; bokeh balls (deep focus is intentional); studio softbox reflections; busy props; flowers, candles, food beyond what is described; orientalist patterns or zellige.
