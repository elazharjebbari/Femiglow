# Returns and refunds policy

> In accordance with Moroccan law 31-08 on consumer protection, you have a right of withdrawal of 7 days from receipt of your order.

## 1. Withdrawal period

### 1.1 Legal duration

You have a period of **7 calendar days** from receipt of your order to exercise your right of withdrawal, **without needing to justify your decision**.

This period begins the day after receipt of the product. If the period expires on a Saturday, Sunday or public holiday, it is extended to the next working day.

### 1.2 Exceptions (law 31-08, art. 37)

The right of withdrawal **does not apply** to products:

- **Unsealed after delivery** and which cannot be returned for hygiene or health protection reasons. A bottle of care product, serum, cream or any product whose protective seal has been opened cannot be returned if it has been used or if the tamper-evident seal has been broken.
- **Personalised** (engraving, dedicated packaging, custom kit).
- **Made to your specifications** or clearly personalised.
- **Likely to deteriorate rapidly**.

Note: if you open a product to test it without consuming it (e.g. a test wipe), withdrawal remains possible — but the product must be in resaleable condition.

## 2. Return conditions

To be accepted, the returned product must be:

- In its **original packaging** (box, case, intact seals)
- With **all accessories** provided (instructions, sample, pouch)
- **Unused** (seal intact for care products)
- Accompanied by the **invoice or order number**

## 3. Return procedure

### Step 1 — Notification

Inform us of your intention to withdraw **before the 7-day period expires**. You may:

- Send an email to: [contact email] (subject: "Withdrawal order #XXX")
- Call: [contact phone]
- Use the dedicated form in your [customer account](/account/orders)

You will receive a confirmation with:
- The return number (RMA)
- The return address
- Packaging instructions

### Step 2 — Returning the product

Return the product within **an additional period of 14 days** following notification. The product travels **at your expense and risk**.

Return address:
```
FemiGlow — Returns Department
[full address]
RMA ref: (your number)
```

**Tips**:
- Use a carrier with tracking (Amana, DHL, CTM, etc.).
- Keep the receipt until refund.
- Pack carefully (the product must arrive intact).

### Step 3 — Receipt and inspection

On receipt, we check within 3 working days that the product meets the return conditions.

- **Compliant** → refund triggered
- **Non-compliant** → you are notified with photos and explanation. The product can be returned to you at your expense or destroyed (at your choice).

### Step 4 — Refund

We process the refund **within 14 days maximum** from receipt of the product.

## 4. Refund terms

### 4.1 Amount refunded

- **Product price**: in full
- **Initial delivery fees**: refunded at the standard rate (if you chose a faster method, only the standard rate is refunded)
- **Return fees**: at your expense (except for defective products, see §6)

### 4.2 Refund method

The refund is processed using the **same payment method** as for the order:

- Bank card: credit to the card (3 to 7 days depending on bank)
- Cash on delivery: bank transfer (IBAN to be provided)
- Bank transfer: transfer to the same account

## 5. Exchange

You may request an **exchange** rather than a refund (subject to stock):
- Exchange for a product of the same value: free (excluding shipping fees)
- Exchange for a product of higher value: difference payable
- Exchange for a product of lower value: difference refunded

The same procedure as a return applies, specifying the desired replacement product.

## 6. Defective or non-compliant product

If the product received is **defective**, **damaged** or **non-compliant with the description**:

### 6.1 Report the issue

Contact us **within 7 days of receipt**:
- Email: [contact email] (subject: "Defective product")
- Attach **photos** of the product + packaging

### 6.2 Proposed solution

Depending on the case:
- **Replacement** with an identical product (free, shipping covered)
- **Full refund** (product + outbound shipping + return shipping)
- **Goodwill gesture** (credit, discount)

This guarantee is in addition to the **legal conformity guarantee** (2 years, law 31-08).

## 7. Special cases

### 7.1 Parcel not delivered

If you have not received your parcel within the announced timeframes + 7 days:
- Check tracking on the carrier's website
- Contact the carrier (pick-up point, local agency)
- If still missing: contact us → investigation + refund after 14 days without news

### 7.2 Parcel damaged on delivery

On receipt:
- **Refuse the parcel** if visibly damaged
- Or **note on the slip**: "Damaged parcel, subject to inspection"
- Photograph immediately and send to [contact email]

### 7.3 Error in the order (wrong product sent)

Contact us within 7 days. We cover all costs (return + dispatch of the correct product).

## 8. Tracking your return

You can track the status of your return in:
- Your account → Orders → Detail
- Statuses: `Request received` → `In transit` → `Received` → `Check OK` → `Refund issued`

## 9. Mediation

In case of persistent disagreement, you may:
- Request mediation from a consumer mediator
- Refer the matter to the courts of Casablanca

See [Terms and conditions of sale §13](/legal/cgv).
