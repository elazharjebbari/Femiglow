## 5. Your rights

You benefit from the following rights (law 09-08, art. 7-13):

### 5.1 Right to information
You are informed before any processing (this policy is its implementation).

### 5.2 Right of access
Obtain a copy of your data held by FemiGlow.

### 5.3 Right of rectification
Correct inaccurate or incomplete data.

### 5.4 Right to object
Object to the processing of your data on legitimate grounds.

### 5.5 Right to erasure
Request deletion of your data, unless required for a legal obligation.

### 5.6 Right to portability
Retrieve your data in a structured, reusable format (JSON / CSV).

### 5.7 Right to withdraw consent
Withdraw your consent at any time for processing that depends on it (marketing, non-essential cookies).

## 6. Data security

FemiGlow implements technical and organisational measures to protect your data:

### 6.1 Technical measures
- TLS 1.3 encryption for communications
- Hashed passwords (bcrypt / Argon2)
- Role-based access control (RBAC)
- Encrypted backups
- Application firewall and anti-DDoS
- Continuous log monitoring

### 6.2 Organisational measures
- Internal data protection charter signed by all staff
- Team training
- Access control
- Regular audits

### 6.3 Breach notification

In the event of a data breach likely to affect your rights, FemiGlow undertakes to notify the CNDP within 72 hours and, where applicable, to inform you directly.
