# FAQ — Customer service

> Answers to the most frequently asked questions. If yours is not here, contact us at [contact email].

## Order

### How do I place an order?

1. Add products to the cart.
2. Click on the cart → "Confirm my order".
3. Enter your delivery information.
4. Choose the payment method.
5. Confirm. You will immediately receive a confirmation email.

### Can I modify my order after confirmation?

As long as the order has not been dispatched (status "In preparation"), you may contact us at [contact email] or [contact phone] for modification or cancellation.

After dispatch, you may exercise your right of withdrawal upon receipt (see [Returns policy](/legal/retours-remboursements)).

### I did not receive a confirmation email, what should I do?

Check:
1. Your junk folders (spam, promotions)
2. The spelling of the email address you entered

If still nothing after 15 minutes, contact us with your phone number or full name.

### Can I order as a guest (without an account)?

Yes, an account is optional. However, an account allows you to track your orders, save your addresses and benefit from loyalty advantages.

## Payment

### What payment methods do you accept?

- Bank card (Visa, Mastercard, CMI) secured via our payment provider
- Cash on delivery (if eligible by zone)
- Bank transfer (on request for orders > 1000 MAD)

### Is my payment secure?

Yes. All transactions are encrypted (TLS 1.3) and processed by our certified banking partner. FemiGlow **never** has access to the full numbers of your card.

### My payment was refused, why?

Possible causes:
- Payment limit reached
- Expired or invalid card
- Incorrect billing address
- Insufficient balance

Contact your bank. You can try again with another card.

### Can I pay in instalments?

Not yet. This option may be offered in partnership with a credit provider. Stay informed via our newsletter.

## Delivery

### What are your delivery times?

- Casablanca, Rabat: 24-72 working hours
- Other major cities: 2-4 days
- Rural zones: 3-7 days

See [Delivery policy](/legal/livraison) for details.

### How can I track my parcel?

From dispatch, you receive an email with the tracking number and a direct link to the carrier's website.

You can also consult the tracking in your [customer account](/account/orders).

### Do you deliver internationally?

Not as standard. For a special request, contact [contact email].

### What if I am not available at delivery?

The carrier leaves a delivery notice. A 2nd attempt is generally made. You can also collect the parcel at the carrier's agency (up to 7 days).

### My parcel is damaged on receipt, what should I do?

1. Refuse or note "subject to inspection" on the slip
2. Photograph immediately
3. Contact us within 7 days at [contact email]

We will arrange a replacement.

## Returns and refunds

### What is the timeframe for returning a product?

**7 days** from receipt (law 31-08, right of withdrawal).

### Are all products returnable?

No. **Unsealed** products (opened bottles) cannot be returned for hygiene reasons, in accordance with article 37 of law 31-08.

See [Returns policy](/legal/retours-remboursements).

### How long does it take to be refunded?

- Return receipt: 3 days for inspection
- Refund: within 14 days after receipt
- Card credit: 3-7 days depending on bank

### Who pays the return fees?

At your expense, unless the product is defective or we made a mistake with the order.

## Products

### How do I choose my product?

- Take our **beauty quiz** for personalised recommendations
- Chat with the **FemiGlow virtual assistant**
- Consult the detailed product pages
- Contact [contact email] for personalised advice

### Are your products natural?

Yes. We select formulations predominantly natural, without long-chain parabens, without phthalates, without occlusive silicones.

The full INCI list is available on every product page.

### Are your products tested on animals?

**No**. None of our products or ingredients is tested on animals. We respect international best practices (Cruelty-Free).

### Are your products suitable for sensitive skin?

Some yes, identified by the "Sensitive" or "Soothing" label. Before any purchase, check the composition. Always perform a patch test (see [Product safety](/legal/securite-produits)).

### Are your products compatible with pregnancy?

Some products carry the "★ Pregnancy Safe" label. In case of doubt, ask your doctor or midwife.

### What to do in case of an allergic reaction?

1. Stop use immediately
2. Rinse with lukewarm water
3. If symptoms persist: consult a doctor
4. Report the incident to [contact email] for cosmetovigilance

See [Product safety warnings](/legal/securite-produits).

## Customer account

### How do I create an account?

Click on "My account" at the top of the page, then "Create an account". Enter email + password.

### I forgot my password

Login page → "Forgot password". You will receive a reset link by email.

### How do I delete my account?

Account → Settings → Delete my account. Or contact [contact email].

Your data will be deleted within 30 days (except for legal obligations: invoices kept for 10 years).

### How do I unsubscribe from the newsletter?

"Unsubscribe" link at the bottom of each email. Or modify your preferences from your account.

## Personal data

### How does FemiGlow protect my data?

See [Privacy policy](/legal/confidentialite). In summary:
- TLS 1.3 encryption
- Password hashing
- Role-restricted access
- Compliance with law 09-08 + CNDP

### Is my data sold to third parties?

**Never**. FemiGlow does not sell or rent your data. Only our logistics, payment and technical subcontractors have access to it, under strict contract.

### How do I exercise my CNDP rights?

Email to [contact email] (subject: "Personal data"). Response within 30 days.

## Cookies

### How do I refuse cookies?

Consent banner on first visit → "Refuse all". You can also modify your preferences at any time via the "Cookie settings" link at the bottom of the page.

### Mandatory cookies: what are they?

Without these cookies, the Site does not function: cart, session, language, security. They do not require your consent.

See [Cookie policy](/legal/cookies).
