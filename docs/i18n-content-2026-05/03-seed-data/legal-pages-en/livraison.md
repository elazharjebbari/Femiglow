# Shipping policy

> Our commitments to ensure your products arrive quickly, in good condition, and without surprises. All deliveries are carried out through recognised logistics partners.

## 1. Delivery zones

FemiGlow delivers throughout the **Kingdom of Morocco**.

### 1.1 Standard zones (fast delivery)

Casablanca · Rabat · Salé · Marrakech · Tangier · Fes · Meknes · Agadir · Tetouan · Oujda · Kenitra · Temara · Mohammedia · Safi · El Jadida

→ Delivery within **24-72 working hours** after dispatch.

### 1.2 Other cities & rural zones

Rest of the territory (other cities, rural centres, outskirts).

→ Delivery within **3-7 working days** after dispatch.

### 1.3 Outside Morocco

No international delivery in V1. A special request may be processed on a case-by-case basis: [contact email].

## 2. Delivery methods

| Method | Description | Timeframe | Fee |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Standard** | Home delivery or pick-up point | 2-5 days | See §3 |
| **Express** | Fast home delivery (Casablanca/Rabat) | 24h | +50 MAD |
| **Pick-up Point** | Collection from partner agency | 2-3 days | -20 MAD |

The method is chosen during cart validation.

## 3. Delivery fees

| Zone | Cart amount | Fee |
|---|---|---|
| Casablanca, Rabat | < 500 MAD | 30 MAD |
| Casablanca, Rabat | ≥ 500 MAD | **Free** |
| Other major cities | < 500 MAD | 40 MAD |
| Other major cities | ≥ 500 MAD | **Free** |
| Rural zones | < 500 MAD | 50 MAD |
| Rural zones | ≥ 500 MAD | **Free** |

The exact amount is calculated and displayed before final order confirmation.

## 4. Processing times

### 4.1 Preparation

Orders are prepared and dispatched:
- **Mon-Fri**: order before 2 p.m. → dispatch the same day
- **Mon-Fri**: order after 2 p.m. → dispatch the next day
- **Saturday**: order before 12 p.m. → dispatch on Monday
- **Sunday & public holidays**: dispatch on the next working day

### 4.2 Transport

Once handed over to the carrier, delivery proceeds according to the timeframes indicated in §1.

### 4.3 Total estimated time

| Zone | Order Monday before 2 p.m. | Estimated receipt |
|---|---|---|
| Casablanca | Dispatch Monday | Tuesday-Wednesday |
| Marrakech | Dispatch Monday | Wednesday-Thursday |
| Rural zone | Dispatch Monday | Following Thursday-Tuesday |

## 5. Tracking your parcel

From dispatch, you receive:
- An email with the **tracking number**
- A direct link to the carrier's website
- An update at each stage (in transit, delivered, etc.)

You can also consult tracking in your [customer account](/account/orders).

## 6. Delivery partners

Depending on zone and chosen method:

- **[Delivery partner]** — Amana, CTM Messagerie, DHL Morocco
- Partner pick-up points

All our partners are vetted and insured.

## 7. Receiving the parcel

### 7.1 Checks at delivery

On delivery:

1. **Check the condition of the parcel** (tears, impact marks).
2. If visible damage:
   - **Refuse** the parcel OR
   - **Note on the slip**: "Damaged parcel, subject to inspection"
3. **Photograph** the parcel (outside + contents if opened).
4. **Sign** the carrier's slip.

### 7.2 Checks after opening

Within 24 hours of receipt, check:
- Content matches the order
- Condition of the products
- Presence of all accessories

In case of issues, contact us within 7 days: [contact email].

## 8. Absence at delivery

### 8.1 Home

If you are absent:
- The carrier leaves a delivery notice
- A 2nd attempt is generally made the next day
- Without response, the parcel is held at the agency for 7 days

### 8.2 Pick-up point

The parcel is kept for **14 days**. Beyond that, it is returned to us. Reshipment fees will be at your expense.

## 9. Incorrect or incomplete address

If you provided an incomplete address and delivery fails:
- First reshipment fees at your expense
- After 2 failed attempts, the order is cancelled and refunded (excluding delivery fees)

Note: please check your address carefully when confirming your order.

## 10. Force majeure and exceptional delays

Timeframes may be affected by:
- Weather conditions (floods, heavy rain)
- Peak periods (Eid, Ramadan, national holidays)
- Carrier strikes
- Pandemic or curfew

In case of significant delay (> 7 days beyond the announced timeframe), you may:
- Wait (with a goodwill gesture: credit or voucher)
- Cancel the order → full refund

## 11. Gifts & curated packaging

For every order, FemiGlow packaging is included free of charge:
- Satin pouch
- Personalised card (optional)
- Sample offered from 300 MAD of purchase

For dedicated **gift packaging** (special occasion), a paid option (+30 MAD) is available in the cart.
