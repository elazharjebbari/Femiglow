# Cookie policy

> This policy explains the use of cookies and trackers on `[site URL]`, in accordance with Moroccan law 09-08 and international best practices (ePrivacy, GDPR).

## 1. What is a cookie?

A cookie is a small text file placed on your device (computer, smartphone, tablet) when you visit a website. It stores information useful for your navigation.

Other similar technologies are also used: local storage, session storage, tracking pixels, web beacons, fingerprinting (limited). For simplicity, we group all these trackers under the term "cookies".

## 2. How FemiGlow uses cookies

We use cookies to:
- Operate the Site (cart, session, language)
- Measure audience and improve the Site
- Personalise your experience
- Offer you relevant advertising

## 3. Types of cookies used

### 3.1 Strictly necessary cookies (always active)

Without these cookies, the Site does not function. They do not require your consent.

| Cookie | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| `femiglow_session` | Session maintenance | Session |
| `femiglow_cart` | Persistent cart | 30 days |
| `femiglow_consent` | Storage of your cookie preferences | 6 months |
| `femiglow_csrf` | Protection against CSRF attacks | Session |
| `femiglow_locale` | Preferred language | 1 year |

### 3.2 Audience measurement cookies (consent required)

Allow us to understand how the Site is used. Data is aggregated and anonymised.

| Cookie | Purpose | Duration | Issuer |
|---|---|---|---|
| `_ga`, `_ga_*` | Google Analytics — visits | 13 months | Google |
| `_gid` | Google Analytics — sessions | 24h | Google |

### 3.3 Marketing / advertising cookies (consent required)

Allow us to measure campaign effectiveness and to personalise advertising.

| Cookie | Purpose | Duration | Issuer |
|---|---|---|---|
| `_fbp` | Facebook Pixel — conversions | 3 months | Meta |
| `_gcl_au` | Google Ads — conversions | 3 months | Google |
| `_gcl_aw` | Google Ads — ad clicks | 3 months | Google |
| `tt_*` | TikTok Pixel | 3 months | TikTok |

### 3.4 Personalisation cookies (consent required)

| Cookie | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| `femiglow_profile` | Beauty preferences (skin type, favourite rituals) | 1 year |
| `femiglow_recos` | AI recommendations cache | 30 days |

## 4. Your choices

### 4.1 Consent banner

On your first visit, a banner allows you to:
- **Accept all**: enable all cookies
- **Reject all**: only essential cookies are enabled
- **Customise**: choose by category

Your choice is stored for 6 months. After expiry, the banner reappears.

### 4.2 Changing your preferences

You can change your preferences at any time:
- "Cookie settings" link at the bottom of each page
- "Manage my cookies" button in the footer
- Customer account → Privacy

### 4.3 Browser configuration

You can also configure your browser to block cookies:

- [Chrome](https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/95647)
- [Safari](https://support.apple.com/guide/safari/manage-cookies-sfri11471/mac)
- [Firefox](https://support.mozilla.org/en-US/kb/enhanced-tracking-protection-firefox-desktop)
- [Edge](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-edge)

Note: blocking all cookies = inability to use the Site (cart, payment).

### 4.4 Specific opt-outs

- Google Analytics: [tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout)
- Google Ads: [adssettings.google.com](https://adssettings.google.com)
- Meta: [facebook.com/settings?tab=ads](https://facebook.com/settings?tab=ads)
- Do Not Track: we respect the browser's DNT header

## 5. Retention

Retention periods are indicated in the tables above. Once expired, cookies are automatically deleted by your browser.
