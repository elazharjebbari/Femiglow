# Product safety warnings and proper use

> Your FemiGlow cosmetics are designed to be gentle, but every skin is unique. Here are the essential precautions before use. Compliant with Moroccan law 24-99 on cosmetic products and with relevant authority recommendations.

## 1. Before first use: perform a tolerance test

### 1.1 Why?

Even natural products can cause allergic reactions or individual intolerances. A patch test reveals any sensitivity **before** application on the entire face or body.

### 1.2 How to perform the patch test?

1. Apply a small amount of the product (hazelnut-sized) to the inside of the elbow or behind the ear.
2. Leave for **24 to 48 hours without rinsing**.
3. Observe for:
   - Redness
   - Itching
   - Warmth
   - Secretion
4. **In case of reaction**: rinse thoroughly, do not use the product, consult a dermatologist if the reaction persists.

## 2. Reading the composition (INCI)

Each FemiGlow product carries its full list of ingredients (INCI — International Nomenclature of Cosmetic Ingredients) on:
- The packaging
- The online product page
- The instructions accompanying the product

### 2.1 Common allergens to be aware of

If you are allergic to any of the following ingredients, always check the composition:

- Fragrances (Fragrance / Parfum) and their 26 allergens (Limonene, Linalool, Citronellol, Geraniol, etc.)
- Preservatives (Methylisothiazolinone, Phenoxyethanol, etc.)
- Lanolin
- Specific essential oils
- Salicylic acid (not recommended during pregnancy)
- Retinol and derivatives (not recommended during pregnancy / breastfeeding)

### 2.2 Certified ingredients

All our products comply with:
- The Moroccan decree implementing law 24-99 on cosmetics
- The European Union positive lists (Annexes of regulation EC no. 1223/2009)
- The absence of prohibited substances (long-chain parabens, phthalates, etc.)

## 3. Audiences requiring special precautions

### 3.1 Pregnant and breastfeeding women

Some active ingredients are not recommended:
- **Retinol** and derivatives
- **Salicylic acid** > 2%
- **Essential oils** (camphor, sage, etc.)
- **Aluminium** in deodorants

→ Consult your doctor or midwife before use. The "★ Pregnancy Safe" label is affixed to our compatible products.

### 3.2 Children

FemiGlow products are formulated for adults. Do not use on children under 12 without paediatric advice.

### 3.3 Sensitive, atopic, reactive skin

- Prefer products labelled "Sensitive" or "Soothing".
- Avoid exfoliants, peels, scrubs.
- Always perform an extended patch test (48-72h).

### 3.4 Skin conditions

In case of eczema, psoriasis, rosacea, severe acne, etc.:
→ **Consult your dermatologist** before any use. FemiGlow offers comfort care, **not therapeutic**.

## 4. Precautions for use

### 4.1 Areas to avoid

- **Eyes**: except specifically designed products (eye contour). Rinse thoroughly with water in case of contact.
- **Mucous membranes**: avoid.
- **Wounds, burns, broken skin**: do not apply.

### 4.2 Sun exposure

Some ingredients (fruit acids, retinol, citrus essential oils) increase **photo-sensitivity**. After their use:
- Avoid sun exposure for 24-48h
- Apply sun protection SPF 30+

### 4.3 Storage

- Store in a **cool and dry** place, away from direct light.
- Close containers tightly after use.
- Respect PAO (Period After Opening) indications: open jar symbol with "6M" = 6 months.

### 4.4 Expiry date

- Check the **minimum durability date** indicated on the product or packaging.
- Beyond that, the product loses its properties and may alter.
- Do not use a product whose texture, smell or colour has visibly changed.

## 5. What to do in case of reaction?

### 5.1 Mild reaction (redness, tingling)

1. Stop use immediately.
2. Rinse the area with lukewarm water without rubbing.
3. Apply a neutral soothing product (thermal water, pure aloe vera gel).
4. Monitor for 24-48h. If the reaction persists or worsens, consult a doctor.

### 5.2 Severe reaction (swelling, hives, breathing difficulty)

Warning: **medical emergency**. Call **15** or go to emergency services immediately.

## 7. Proper use by product type

### 7.1 Serums

- Apply to clean, dry skin.
- A few drops are enough.
- Pat in, do not rub.
- To be used before moisturiser.

### 7.2 Moisturisers

- Quantity: hazelnut-sized for the face.
- Massage in circular upward motions.
- Morning and/or evening.

### 7.3 Oils

- 2-3 drops are sufficient.
- Warm between palms before application.
- As a finishing step in the routine.

### 7.4 Scrubs and exfoliants

- **1-2 times per week maximum**.
- Not on broken, irritated or sensitive skin.
- Always follow with hydration.

### 7.5 Masks

- 10-20 min depending on instructions.
- 1-2 times per week.
- Rinse thoroughly.
